Compared to state-of-the-art local learning meth-\nods that require observing post-synaptic neuronal outputs to optimise\nsynaptic weights via error-based gradient descent, Forward Projection\noperates under a stricter constraint,ﬁtting weights using just the pre-\nsynaptic neuronal activity and labels.\nThe target generation function proposed here for Forward Pro-\njection promotes input and label encoding in neural membrane\npotentials (see section S.8 in SI). Joint encoding of labels with pre-\nsynaptic activity alleviates the degenerate neural activity which results\nfrom local modelling of simple label projections, whilst avoiding the\nintroduction of uninformative noise (see section S.6 in SI). In this\nanalysis, a simple non-linearity was employed for target generation.\nThe utility of more complex nonlinearities for target generation is a\nsubject for further research.\nExplainability of neural network models is an important limitation\nin decision-critical ﬁelds such as biomedicine, where errors such as\nconfounded decisions may lead to signi ﬁcant consequences\n35.F P -\ntrained layers may be interpreted without downstream information,\nproviding insight into model reasoning in hidden layers. FP inter-\npretation yielded informative outputs in three model architectures,\nidentifying clinically salient features in ECG sequences and OCT ima-\nges.
